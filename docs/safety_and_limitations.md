# Safety, scope and limitations

ONCO-DYNAMICS X is a university educational and research prototype. It must not screen for breast cancer; interpret an individual's scan, pathology slide, genetic result or symptoms; diagnose, stage or prognosticate for a patient; select, rank, prescribe or dose cancer therapy; or replace clinical review and validated tools.

The baseline is dimensionless, synthetic and not biologically calibrated. It lacks patient-specific anatomy, validated parameter estimates, pharmacokinetics, clonal heterogeneity, complete immune phenotypes, ECM mechanics and clinical outcome validation.

A visual rendering is not evidence of biological accuracy. Machine-learning output is not evidence of causality. Retrospective performance may fail in new institutions, scanners, treatment settings or populations.

Future clinical research requires appropriate institutional oversight, consent or waiver, data-use agreements, privacy safeguards and independent validation. A clinical device pathway would require separate regulatory, quality and safety work beyond this academic project.
